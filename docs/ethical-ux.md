# Ethical interaction psychology

PQVPN applies behavioural and cognitive principles to reduce uncertainty, not
to manufacture compliance. Every control surface should help a person answer:

1. **Where am I?** Use stable navigation, explicit modes and a visible system
   state.
2. **What will happen?** Label the concrete effect, boundary and risk before an
   action.
3. **Did it work?** Return immediate, proportional feedback based on real node
   state rather than decorative success.
4. **Can I recover?** Prefer reversible actions, preserve a safe route back and
   explain destructive operations before they occur.
5. **What remains private?** State what stays local, what crosses the network
   and what is retained.

Visual hierarchy, progressive disclosure, recognition over recall, bounded
goals and calm reward feedback are encouraged. Expert controls remain
available without overwhelming the primary path. Accessibility, reduced
motion, keyboard operation and readable contrast are product requirements.

The following are prohibited:

- fake urgency, scarcity, social proof or security state;
- streaks, variable-ratio rewards or loss aversion designed to compel use;
- preselected consent, disguised advertising or obstructed exit;
- shame, fear or inflated anonymity claims;
- rewards tied to disclosure of personal data or weakening protection.

Pixel Commons follows the same rule: PQ communicate local progress, never
financial value or status pressure. Character bubbles disappear and are not
persisted. A satisfying animation may celebrate an evidenced outcome; it may
not substitute for one.
