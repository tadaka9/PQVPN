Stockholm mainnet exit policy

Experimental service. PQVPN has not received an independent security audit. Availability, capacity and fitness for any purpose are not guaranteed.

Endpoint

45.248.37.87:9090 provides authenticated relay and user-space Internet egress over UDP. Discovery starts at the separately operated Helsinki bootstrap. The registry publishes IPv4 only and TCP port 9090 is closed. Private, loopback, link-local and reserved destinations are rejected; outbound SMTP is blocked; concurrent user-space flows are bounded.

Acceptable use

Do not use this node for malware delivery, phishing, credential theft, unauthorized access, denial of service, spam, exploitation, harassment, illegal material, or infringement of third-party rights. Automated high-volume scanning is prohibited.

Operations and abuse

The operator may rate-limit or block traffic to protect the service or respond to credible abuse. Report suspected abuse through the PQVPN issue tracker. Include timestamps and destination addresses without publishing secrets or personal data.
