Helsinki bootstrap policy

Experimental service. PQVPN has not received an independent security audit. Availability and capacity are not guaranteed.

Endpoints

65.108.251.110:9090 and [2a01:4f9:c012:add9::1]:9090 provide discovery and authenticated session bootstrap over UDP. The host and listener were observed with public IPv4/IPv6 on 17 October 2026. It does not provide public Internet egress. TCP port 9090 is closed.

Acceptable use

Do not use this node for malware delivery, credential theft, unauthorized access, denial of service, spam, exploitation, harassment, illegal material, or infringement of third-party rights.

Logging

This endpoint follows the no-activity-logs policy: protocol output is discarded and connection or traffic history is not retained.

Operations and abuse

The operator may rate-limit or block traffic to protect the service. Report suspected abuse through the PQVPN issue tracker.

Policy updated October 17, 2026.
