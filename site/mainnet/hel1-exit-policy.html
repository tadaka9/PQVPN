Helsinki community relay policy

Experimental service. PQVPN has not received an independent security audit. Availability, capacity and fitness for any purpose are not guaranteed.

Endpoint

45.248.37.87:9090 provides bootstrap and relay service over the published PQVPN UDP protocol. The registry currently publishes IPv4 only. TCP port 9090 and public egress are disabled; the registry will be updated before either capability is advertised.

Acceptable use

Do not use this node for malware delivery, phishing, credential theft, unauthorized access, denial of service, spam, exploitation, harassment, distribution of illegal material, or infringement of third-party rights. Automated high-volume scanning and deliberate attempts to degrade the service are prohibited.

Operations and abuse

The operator may rate-limit, block or remove access when necessary to protect the node or respond to credible abuse. Report suspected abuse through the PQVPN issue tracker. Include timestamps, destination addresses and enough technical detail to investigate without publishing secrets or personal data.

Policy updated October 13, 2026.
