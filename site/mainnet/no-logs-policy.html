No-activity-logs policy

The Helsinki bootstrap and Stockholm mainnet exit do not retain logs of VPN activity.

Data not logged

The public-node services discard protocol output. They do not retain source peer addresses, connection timestamps, session identifiers, destination addresses or ports, DNS queries, transferred content, byte counts, or traffic history. Firewall rules do not log forwarded or egress traffic. There is no analytics or telemetry pipeline.

Minimal operational state

Each node retains its own private identity and the minimum TOFU public-key trust state needed to recognize previously seen peers. This state is not a browsing or traffic history. The in-memory session and flow tables disappear when a session ends or the service restarts.

Separate host-security records

The hosts retain administrative security records that do not describe VPN use: SSH authentication and fail2ban events, audit records for privileged changes to service/configuration files, operating-system package activity, and release-updater results. These records protect the machines and are not combined with VPN traffic data.

Limits

This policy describes the two endpoints listed in the official registry. A third-party node has its own operator and policy. Network providers can observe traffic reaching their infrastructure, and an endpoint contacted through the exit can observe the exit IP.

Policy effective October 13, 2026.
